NPC: Let me tell you about my morning in San Francisco.
NPC: I wake up. Normally I take a Waymo downtown, but today I walk over to Golden Gate Park. 
NPC: Early in the morning, before 8:00, the park is bathed in a heavy fog. The mist hangs in the air and you can feel it on your cheeks as you walk. 
NPC: The park is hilly and thick with green of the grass and the trees. The birds chirp from far out of sight. In that moment, the world feels still. The dew will leave your sneakers wet if you walk across the meadows. Backpack on, walking up a slight incline in the grey and green, feels like you might be climbing up a misty mountain top somewhere far away.
NPC: I walk all the way through the park to its very south-eastern corner, and keep walking east through Cole Valley. 
NPC: The fog quickly ends there, from one block to the next, and some of the most picturesque Victorians in the city have their colors revealed by the soft sunlight. In the center of Cole Valley is a cafe called Wooden Coffeehouse that has the most comfortable, finely curved handmade wooden chairs in the city. 
NPC: I purchase a cappuccino, which is rich and creamy, and I think about how much I love being there.
NPC: Across the street is a streetcar stop. It moves quickly and smoothly and carves its way straight through the hills in the center of the city. 
NPC: When it drops me off at my workplace among all the high-rises downtown, there's not a drop of fog in the air, just the pure great sun of California that I had been told about and imagined long before I first arrived here.
